About the Project

0ero Gravity Art reimagines Bauhaus design principles for the blockchain age, creating an immersive, interactive showcase for Bauhaus-inspired NFT collections.

By liberating geometric forms from the constraints of gravity, we create a new dimension of artistic expression that honors the Bauhaus legacy while embracing web3 innovation.
